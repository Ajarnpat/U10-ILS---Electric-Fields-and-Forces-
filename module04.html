Guided enquiry

Rotate a 3-D conductor model to inspect surface charge, zero internal field, and perpendicular field lines.

  1. Drag the conductor to rotate it in three dimensions.
  2. Inspect the field lines from several viewpoints. Check that they leave the surface at right angles.
  3. Use the model to explain why the electric field inside a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium is zero.
Electrostatic equilibrium: Einside conductor = 0
