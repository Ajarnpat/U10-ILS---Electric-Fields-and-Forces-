Guided enquiry

Investigate capacitance, plate geometry, dielectric effects, uniform fields, charge and stored energy.

  1. Vary plate area, separation and dielectric constant. Observe how capacitance responds.
  2. Compare the number/direction of field arrows between plates with the ideal uniform-field model.
  3. Use C ∝ κA/d and U = ½C(ΔV)² to make predictions before changing a control.
C = Q / ΔV    •    C = κε₀A / d    •    UC = ½C(ΔV)²
