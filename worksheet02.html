Key Learning Summary

Know / understand / be able to do:
  • Charge is conserved for an isolated system.
  • Charging normally involves electron transfer.
  • Polarization is charge separation; grounding connects a system to a much larger approximately neutral system.

Core relationship: Qsystem, before = Qsystem, after
